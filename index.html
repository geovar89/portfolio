CASE 02 / MOBILITYPOC / INITIATIVE

Removing friction from the rental experience.

Skip the Counter · Autohellas Group

Product focusPre-check-inIdentity & paymentsMVP definitionNot presented as launched

01 — Challenge

Redesign the traditional pickup journey while considering identity checks, payment flows and operational dependencies.

02 — Product choices

Define a staged POC journey across pre-check-in, optional extras, payment, verification, e-signature and handover.

03 — My contribution

Collaborated with IT, business owners and vendors on scope, requirements and trade-offs between customer value and integration complexity.

04 — Outcome

Defined a structured concept, customer journey and POC scope. Adoption, financial and conversion impact are not claimed.
